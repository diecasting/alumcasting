## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How do you guarantee porosity control on massive 5000T structural castings?

A: Large-scale castings are highly susceptible to gas and shrinkage porosity. We counteract this by deploying advanced real-time shot-monitoring systems, vacuum-assisted die casting valves, and optimized thermal management across the die faces. Every critical structural run undergoes non-destructive testing via our specialized X-ray equipment to ensure inner core density.

### Q2: Can we achieve weldability and heat-treatment properties on parts from this 5000T line?

A: Yes. Traditional high-pressure castings cannot undergo T6 heat treatment due to trapped gas blisters. However, by leveraging our advanced [semi-solid die casting for heat-treatable aluminum](/semi-solid-die-casting-heat-treatable-aluminum/) alongside structural vacuum processes, we produce highly ductile, pore-free parts that are fully weldable and perfect for T6 hardening structural applications.

### Q3: What are the primary logistical steps for shipping large-scale components to Europe or North America?

A: Over 70% of our production is exported. For mega-scale parts like EV battery trays, we utilize custom-engineered returnable or export-safe heavy industrial packaging to prevent warping during sea transit. We work seamlessly with global freight forwarders to provide DDP, CIF, or FOB terms, handling all customs clearance documentation smoothly from China.
