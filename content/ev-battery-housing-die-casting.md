# Aluminum EV Battery Housing Die Casting Review

EV battery housings combine structural, sealing, thermal, machining and assembly requirements. AlumCasting supports engineering and sourcing reviews for aluminum housing and tray projects, including casting feasibility, die and process planning, CNC post-machining and project-specific inspection.

[Review Your EV Battery Housing Project](/contact/)

## Battery Housing Design Requirements

The drawing review should define:

- Battery module and pack envelope
- Structural loads and crash-interface requirements
- Mounting points and assembly sequence
- Sealing faces and pressure-tightness limits
- Cooling-channel, coolant-interface or thermal-contact areas
- Electrical grounding and EMI considerations
- Fastener, insert and service-access features
- Corrosion protection and cleaning requirements

The housing concept must be validated by the customer and applicable vehicle or pack program. This page does not claim compliance with an unspecified battery safety standard.

## Aluminum Die Casting and 5000T Project Review

Large battery trays may require a project review for [large-scale aluminum die casting](/large-scale-5000t-aluminum-die-casting-factory-china/) or 5000T-class machine capacity. Tonnage alone is not sufficient; the review must include:

- Part envelope and cast weight
- Projected area and shot capacity
- Die size, weight, tie-bar and platen requirements
- Filling distance and wall sections
- Ejection, handling and machining access
- Required equipment owner, operator and host factory
- Current asset availability

The 5000T business topic is retained, but ownership, operator, installation and current aluminum-production status remain subject to written project qualification.

## DFM for Battery Housings

Early DFM should review:

- Main structural ribs and load paths
- Wall-thickness transitions
- Flatness and sealing-face stability
- Casting distortion and machining allowance
- Datum strategy and fixture access
- Gating, overflow, venting and possible vacuum assistance
- Thermal balance in large die sections
- Machining exposure of critical sealing areas
- Handling and packaging constraints

The final design should not rely on generic rules for wall thickness or tolerance without checking the actual geometry, alloy and process.

## Alloy and Process Selection

The aluminum alloy should be selected for the housing requirements, not from a single generic recommendation. A380 and ADC12 may be evaluated for HPDC components, while A356 or semi-solid alternatives may be considered when heat treatment, ductility or different porosity requirements are important.

[Review aluminum die casting](/aluminum-die-casting/) or [semi-solid casting considerations](/semi-solid-die-casting-heat-treatable-aluminum/).

## Tooling, Trials and CNC Machining

The tooling plan should connect the casting design to machining:

- Die concept and mechanical movements
- Thermal management and venting
- Trial sample scope
- Sealing-face and mounting-face machining
- Datums, fixtures and workholding
- Distortion control after casting and heat treatment
- Surface finishing and assembly interfaces

[Explore die casting tooling](/die-casting-tooling/) or [CNC machining for castings](/precision-cnc-machining/).

## Inspection and Leak Testing

The approved inspection plan should define:

- Material and process records
- Critical dimensional and GD&T checks
- CMM or other measurement methods
- X-ray or CT where specified
- Pressure or leak-test medium, pressure and duration
- Leak-rate limit, stabilization and sampling
- Surface finish and coating checks
- Nonconformance and corrective-action process

No universal 100% leak test, zero-leak result or full-batch X-ray coverage is assumed. [Review porosity and inspection planning](/porosity-control-x-ray-inspection-castings/).

## Supplier Qualification for EV Battery Programs

Before releasing tooling, the supplier package should clarify:

- Manufacturing route and equipment assignment
- Asset owner, operator and production site
- Process-control plan and inspection methods
- RFQ assumptions and exclusions
- Heat treatment, machining and finishing responsibilities
- Packaging, cleanliness and traceability requirements
- Change-control and approval process

This qualification can be combined with [automotive die casting project review](/automotive-die-casting-parts/) and [manufacturing capability assessment](/manufacturing-capabilities/).

## What to Include in an EV Battery Housing RFQ

Provide:

- 3D model and 2D drawing with GD&T
- Aluminum alloy and material condition
- Expected annual volume and launch stages
- Battery envelope, part size and cast weight
- Structural and crash-interface requirements
- Sealing and pressure/leak-test criteria
- Cooling and electrical-interface requirements
- Machining, finishing and assembly scope
- Inspection methods and documentation
- Packaging, handling and project timing

Incomplete RFQs can still be reviewed. Missing or customer-controlled requirements are identified before a production scope is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What information is needed for an EV battery housing RFQ?

Provide the 3D model, 2D drawing with GD&T, alloy, battery envelope, expected volume, load and sealing requirements, cooling or mounting interfaces, machining scope, leak-test criteria and inspection plan.

### Can a large aluminum battery housing be die cast in one piece?

A one-piece or integrated casting may be evaluated when the part envelope, projected area, shot capacity, die design and machine route fit the proposed process. The final route must be confirmed against the drawing and written asset qualification.

### How is battery-housing leak tightness defined?

The pressure or leak-test method, test medium, pressure, stabilization time, leak limit and sampling plan must be defined for the specific housing and customer specification. No universal zero-leak result is assumed.

### How should porosity be controlled in an EV battery housing?

Porosity control should connect alloy and melt quality, die and gating design, venting or vacuum strategy, thermal balance, machining exposure and project-specific inspection acceptance criteria.

### Can casting be followed by CNC machining and finishing?

Yes. Casting allowance, datums, fixture access, sealing faces, fastener features, finishing and inspection should be reviewed together so the finished housing meets the approved drawing.
