## Why Choose Aluminum Die Casting for EV Battery Trays?

Aluminum is the material of choice for EV battery enclosures due to its exceptional strength-to-weight ratio and thermal conductivity. Our casting process ensures:

- **Lightweight Performance** Reducing vehicle weight to maximize driving range without compromising structural integrity.
- **Complex Geometries** Advanced die designs allow for integrated cooling channels and mounting brackets in a single cast.
- **EMI Shielding** Inherent electromagnetic interference shielding properties protect sensitive battery electronics.
- **Precision Tolerances** Critical for maintaining the air-tightness and leak-proof standards required for battery safety.
