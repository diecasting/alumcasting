# Automotive CNC Machining and Supplier Qualification

Automotive CNC programs require the machining route, datum strategy, fixture, inspection and documentation to be qualified together. This page covers cast-and-machined aluminum components and the supplier evidence a purchasing or engineering team should request.

[Review Automotive CNC Requirements](/contact/)

## Start With the Drawing and Process Route

The review begins with:

- 3D model and 2D drawing with GD&T
- Material or casting condition
- Critical safety, sealing, structural and assembly features
- Machining allowance and near-net geometry
- Datum strategy and fixture access
- Expected annual volume and launch stages
- Surface, cleaning and finishing requirements
- Inspection, documentation and approval scope

The proposed process should match the actual drawing. A general machining claim does not establish capability for every automotive feature.

## Feature-Level Tolerance Review

Tolerance should be reviewed feature by feature, not as one standard number for the entire part. The assessment should consider:

- Drawing requirement and datum reference
- Material and casting condition
- Fixture stability and workholding
- Machine assignment and cutting strategy
- Tool wear and process control
- Temperature and measurement uncertainty
- Inspection accessibility

The approved capability must be demonstrated for the specified feature and process route. A blanket ±0.01 mm or other numeric claim is not published here without project evidence.

## Casting and CNC Machining

For cast-and-machined components, the casting and machining plans should be developed together:

- Casting allowance and stock distribution
- Stable casting datums
- Clamping and distortion control
- Critical faces, bores, threads and sealing features
- Feature sequencing and re-fixturing
- Deburring, cleaning and surface protection

[Review CNC machining for castings](/precision-cnc-machining/) or [automotive die-casting requirements](/automotive-die-casting-parts/).

## Cpk, SPC and Process Capability

Cpk and SPC are meaningful only when the characteristic, sample plan and process are defined. A valid capability review should establish:

- Which dimensions or functional characteristics require capability data
- The measurement method and uncertainty
- The production stage and sample size
- Process stability and control limits
- The required acceptance criterion
- The response when the process is not capable

A Cpk target should not be implied before these inputs are agreed and representative data are available.

## IATF 16949 and Supplier Qualification

An IATF 16949 request is a supplier-qualification question, not a marketing badge. Before accepting a certificate statement, confirm:

- Exact legal name of the certificate holder
- Certificate number, scope and production site
- Current validity and surveillance status
- Whether the product and process are covered
- The legal right to reference the certificate under the relevant brand
- Any customer-specific approval or audit requirement

This page does not independently establish that AlumCasting or every manufacturing partner holds a current IATF 16949 certificate for the requested scope. [Review manufacturing capabilities](/manufacturing-capabilities/).

## PPAP, FAI and Documentation

Documentation scope is defined during the RFQ and supplier qualification. Depending on the project, requirements may include:

- Drawing and change records
- Material and process records
- First-article inspection
- Dimensional and GD&T results
- Process-flow and control-plan documents
- Measurement-system analysis
- Capability data where applicable
- Nonconformance and corrective-action records

A PPAP level, report package or full traceability requirement should be stated explicitly and approved for the specific part. PPAP Level 1-5 support is not claimed as an automatic service.

## Automotive Component Review

Potential aluminum component categories include housings, covers, brackets, motor components, thermal-management parts and other cast-and-machined assembly interfaces. Suitability depends on load, geometry, volume, material, tolerance, inspection and validation requirements.

These are application categories for review, not claims of completed Tier-1 or customer-specific programs.

## What to Include in an Automotive CNC RFQ

Provide:

- 3D model and 2D drawing with GD&T
- Material or casting condition
- Expected annual volume and launch ramp
- Critical characteristics and datum strategy
- Fixture, machining and surface requirements
- Inspection method and report format
- PPAP, FAI, SPC or Cpk request
- Customer standards and supplier-qualification requirements
- Packaging, traceability and project timing

Incomplete RFQs can still be reviewed. Missing and customer-controlled requirements are identified before capability or production scope is confirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this page prove that AlumCasting is IATF 16949 certified?

No. IATF 16949 requests must be reviewed against the certificate holder, exact scope, validity, production site and legal right to reference the certificate. Certification is not inferred from this page or from a customer's requirement.

### How are automotive CNC tolerances confirmed?

Tolerances are reviewed feature by feature against the drawing, material, datum strategy, fixture, machine assignment and measurement method. The approved capability must be demonstrated for the specific project.

### When is Cpk or SPC applicable?

Cpk and SPC require a defined characteristic, stable process, valid sample plan, control method and sufficient data. The project must specify the characteristic and acceptance method before capability can be reported.

### Can PPAP or first-article documentation be provided?

The required documentation level, report format, sample plan and approval responsibilities are defined during supplier qualification. PPAP Level 1-5, full dimensional reports or traceability are not assumed as standard for every order.

### What information is needed for an automotive CNC RFQ?

Provide the 3D model, 2D drawing with GD&T, material or casting condition, annual volume, critical features, datum strategy, finishing, inspection scope, PPAP/documentation request and target production stage.
