## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does your 400+ CNC capacity benefit my automotive project?

Scale ensures supply chain insurance. With 400+ CNC units online, we can dedicate standalone production cells exclusively to your program. This redundancy eliminates queue bottlenecks common in smaller machine shops, facilitates flawless preventative tool-wear changes, and comfortably absorbs sudden OEM production ramp-ups.

### Do you have dedicated equipment and safety protocols for magnesium machining?

Yes. Magnesium micro-chips and dust are highly combustible, requiring strict engineering isolation. We have physically segregated CNC workzones equipped with wet-type dust extractors, explosion-proof light fixtures, and customized synthetic coolants. Our operators are explicitly trained in magnesium handling to guarantee continuous, hazardous-free operations.

### How do you maintain structural accuracy across such a massive fleet of machines?

Every CNC unit follows a hard-scheduled preventive maintenance routine calibrated via laser interferometers to correct geometric deviations. In parallel, our Statistical Process Control (SPC) software samples active station dimensions directly from the line, ensuring machine capability indices (Cpk) consistently trend above 1.33 for strict compliance.

### Can your machining department handle large-scale structural parts like EV battery trays?

Our facility layout links our high-tonnage 5000T cold-chamber die casting line directly to an array of large-travel horizontal and multi-axis CNC milling machines. This specialized line is structurally optimized to mill extensive surface areas, profile complex perimeter gasket channels, and thread mounting bosses on complex EV battery trays or motor housings while holding global flatness requirements.
