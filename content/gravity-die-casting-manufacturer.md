# Gravity Die Casting: Why "Slow and Steady" Wins the Strength Race

*By Senior Tooling Engineer | 20+ Years in China's Casting Industry Hubs*

In the high-speed world of modern manufacturing, where "instant" is the default setting, Gravity Die Casting (GDC) might seem like a relic to the uninitiated. But as a gravity die casting manufacturer who has spent two decades debugging molds in the humid heat of Dongguan, I can tell you: gravity is the most reliable partner you'll ever have.

While High-Pressure Die Casting (HPDC) is great for thin walls and speed, GDC is where we go when a part needs to *work* under extreme stress. If your component is the heart of an engine or a critical structural mount in [automotive casting applications](/automotive-die-casting-parts/), you don't just want it fast—you want it flawless.

## The Full Lifecycle: Beyond the Pour

What many buyers overlook is that the pour is just the beginning. As shown in the process map above, a true expert manufacturer provides a vertically integrated path. Because GDC parts have such high density, they are the perfect candidates for complex post-processing.

I've seen projects fail because a client used one shop for casting and another for machining. At AlumCasting, we control the grain structure during the pour, which ensures that when we move to [high-tolerance automotive CNC machining](/high-tolerance-automotive-cnc-machining/) *(page deferred — not in Batch 1)*, the metal doesn't "creep" or deform.

> I've stood by GDC machines at 2 AM, watching the aluminum pour like liquid silk. Unlike HPDC, which forces metal in with violent speed, gravity lets the metal breathe, reducing the turbulence that causes those hidden, project-killing bubbles.

## The Secret Sauce: Density and T6 Heat Treatment

The biggest reason clients come to us is for structural integrity. Because GDC uses a slower filling rate, the level of entrapped air is significantly lower. This is critical because it allows us to perform [T6 heat treatment](/t6-heat-treatment-semi-solid-die-casting-aluminum/) *(page deferred — not in Batch 1)* without fear.

You simply can't do this with standard HPDC without risking surface blisters. By choosing a manufacturer that understands the nuance of [preventing blistering during T6](/prevent-blistering-aluminum-t6-heat-treatment/) *(page deferred — not in Batch 1)*, you unlock mechanical properties that high-pressure parts can only dream of.

### Gravity Casting (GDC)

- Lower porosity, superior density.
- Full heat treatment compatibility (T4-T6).
- Ideal for [bridge tooling and low volume](/bridge-tooling-low-volume-aluminum-die-casting-guide/) *(page deferred — not in Batch 1)*.

### Pressure Casting (HPDC)

- High speed for massive volume.
- Exceptional for ultra-thin walls.
- Higher initial [mold investment](/cost-down-dfm-design-aluminum-die-casting-molds/) *(page deferred — not in Batch 1)*.

## Technical Capabilities: Process, Alloys, Tolerance & Production

### The Gravity Casting Process, Step by Step

Gravity die casting (permanent mold casting) pours molten aluminum into a reusable steel mold under gravity alone. The controlled, repeatable fill produces castings with lower porosity and higher density than sand casting — which is why it is the preferred route for structural and pressure-tested parts. The typical sequence is mold preheating, pouring, controlled solidification, ejection, trimming, heat treatment (T4–T6), machining, then inspection.

### Aluminum Alloy Options

- **A356 / A357** — best strength-to-weight ratio and elongation; fully heat-treatable (T6); ideal for structural and safety components.
- **A380 / ADC12** — excellent castability and pressure tightness for housings, enclosures, and chassis parts.
- **A319 / A390** — high-temperature and wear applications.

We match the alloy to your loading, corrosion, and cost targets instead of forcing a one-size-fits-all material.

### Tolerance Capability

Gravity die castings typically hold **±0.3 mm** on critical dimensions, with tighter features achievable through post-cast CNC machining. Draft angles, wall-thickness transitions, and gate positions are engineered in the tooling stage so tolerances stay stable across the full production run.

### Production Capability

From bridge-tooling prototypes to high-volume production, our gravity casting line supports a wide range of part sizes and annual volumes, with in-house heat treatment, machining, and inspection (CMM / X-ray) so the casting arrives ready for assembly.
