# Contact AlumCasting

Share your component drawing, expected volume and critical requirements so the project can be reviewed for aluminum die casting, magnesium die casting, tooling, CNC machining, finishing and inspection scope.

[Send Your RFQ Details](/contact/#send-your-project-requirements)

## What to Include in Your RFQ

A useful first review normally includes:

- 3D model and 2D drawing with GD&T where available
- Exact alloy and governing material standard
- Expected annual volume and initial order quantity
- Component function and operating environment
- Critical walls, machined features and sealing areas
- Surface finish, coating or plating requirements
- Inspection, documentation or customer-standard requirements
- Target sample and production timing

An incomplete package can still be used for an initial discussion. Missing requirements are identified before tooling, production scope or a final quotation is confirmed.

## RFQ Review and Quotation Basis

The review connects the drawing to the proposed manufacturing route. Depending on the project, the response may identify:

- Assumptions and information still required
- Feasible process and tooling questions
- Critical dimensions, sealing or inspection needs
- Finishing and packaging requirements
- Quotation inclusions and exclusions
- Requirements that need customer confirmation

No lead time, tool life, tolerance, certification, inspection coverage or quality outcome is guaranteed before the project requirements are reviewed and approved.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What files should I send with a die casting RFQ?

Send a 3D model and a 2D drawing with critical dimensions and GD&T where available. Include the alloy or material specification, annual volume, surface finish, machining requirements and any inspection or customer standard.

### Can AlumCasting review an incomplete RFQ?

Yes. A concept model, part function and expected volume can support an initial feasibility discussion. Missing drawing, tooling, inspection or customer requirements are identified before a production scope and final quotation are confirmed.

### What information has the greatest effect on feasibility?

Alloy, part geometry, wall sections, projected area, annual volume, critical tolerances, sealing or leak requirements, finishing and inspection scope usually have the largest effect. Tooling status and target timing also affect the review.

### Does an RFQ confirm certification or documentation scope?

No. Certificate holder, scope, validity, production site and customer-specific documentation must be verified for the proposed project. PPAP, traceability, inspection coverage or other requirements should be stated explicitly in the RFQ.
